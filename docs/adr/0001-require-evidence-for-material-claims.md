# Require evidence for material claims

Every material factual Claim in an Investigation must be traceable to immutable Evidence Records captured from identified Sources; conclusions that go beyond that evidence must be labeled as inferences and cite their inputs. Relationships distinguish supporting, contradicting, and contextual evidence instead of reducing provenance to mutable URLs. This favors auditability and honest uncertainty over fluent but unsupported coverage, and makes provenance a first-class concern across ingestion, retrieval, generation, persistence, and evaluation.
